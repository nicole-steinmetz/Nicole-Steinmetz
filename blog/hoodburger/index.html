Hoodburger: a Shopify site and loyalty program for a 5-store Perth burger chain

NNicole Steinmetz · Web designer and developer
  • Shopify
The brief What I built The Round Table How I worked

The brief

Hoodburger is a Perth smash-burger chain with five stores. They needed a site that did more than show a menu. It had to be fast on mobile, easy for customers to find the nearest store and order, and ready to grow into a loyalty program and franchising.

What I built

A custom Shopify site on the Dawn theme, with 15 purpose-built page templates. They cover home, menu, locations, rewards, catering, careers, FAQs, order now, contact and the legal pages. Store details, ordering and the allergy information are easy to reach from any page.

The loyalty program: The Round Table

I set up their Marsello loyalty program with a Yellowstone Castle / RPG theme. Members start as Wastelanders and can climb to Yellowstone Citizen and Wizard, earning more points per dollar at each tier. I also set up the birthday cheeseburger and the launch double-points period. The result feels like part of the brand, not a bolted-on app.

How I worked

I designed in Figma, generated the front-end with Claude, then refined and shipped it as Shopify Liquid in Cursor. That let a small studio deliver a fully custom build quickly. After launch I ran an SEO and structured-data pass, adding FAQ schema and fixing page titles and headings.
