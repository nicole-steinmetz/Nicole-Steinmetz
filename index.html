How We Build

Every project starts with the same question: what does this business actually need? Squarespace for simple, low-maintenance sites that clients can manage themselves. Shopify for businesses where selling is the core function. Custom builds for briefs that need more motion, interaction, or flexibility than a platform can comfortably handle — usually on Cloudflare, so hosting, domain and security sit in one account the business owns, with Vercel or Netlify where the brief calls for it. I choose the right system for the job, not the most fashionable one.
