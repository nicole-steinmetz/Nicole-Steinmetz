Pricing — Cloudflare

Pay for the site,
not a subscription.

Most platforms charge a plan fee every month for as long as your site exists. This one doesn’t — hosting, security and your domain sit in a single account in your name, and the only thing you keep paying for is the domain. You’re not trading down to get it, either: it’s quick wherever your customers are and protected by default. Costs a bit more to build, next to nothing to keep. Build your estimate below.
