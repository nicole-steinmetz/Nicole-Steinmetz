How a build runs

Discover Design Launch Training
  1. 1 Step 1

    Discovery & Scope

    We start with a chat about your business, what the site needs to do and when you’d like it live. Before design begins, your images and near-final copy come in, and we lock in your pages together so your build is scheduled around something real.

    Scope locked
  2. 2 Step 2

    Desktop Design & Build

    I design and build the full site for desktop first, so please review it on a laptop or desktop at this stage. We use your rounds of changes to get it exactly right, and nothing moves to mobile until you’ve signed desktop off.

    Desktop signed off
  3. 3 Step 3

    Mobile & Launch

    Now’s the time to grab your phone. I optimise every page for mobile and you get a round of touch-ups. Once the balance is settled, the site moves into your account and your domain is pointed live. It can take up to 48 hours to show everywhere.

    Live
  4. 4 Step 4

    Training

    Once you’re live, we do a one-on-one session on your own site so you feel confident making updates yourself.

    Yours to keep
