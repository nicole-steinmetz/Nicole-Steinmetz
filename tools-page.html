How the pieces fit

Platform

The software you log into to edit your site.Squarespace and Shopify include hosting.

Hosting

The computers that show your site to visitors.A custom-coded site needs its own host.

Domain

Your web address, such as yourbusiness.com.au.

DNS

The address book that tells the internet where your website and your email live.

One genuine gotcha: moving DNS to Cloudflare or Netlify can interrupt your email if the MX records aren't copied across first. It is a one-time step and carries no risk when handled properly, so I list your existing records before anything changes. See the FAQ on domain and email access.

Platforms

A custom build is hand-written code, so it needs a host. These are the three I use. None has a visual editor, and I keep access to your site after launch for further changes. See the FAQ on updating your site after launch.

The five platforms at a glance

Recommendation: choose by the job. Squarespace if you'll edit the site yourself. Shopify if selling is the business. A custom build when a template can't do what you need.

Genuine strength, core compromise and when to avoid each platform
Platform Genuine strength Core compromise Avoid when
Squarespace Fast, low-maintenance sites you can update yourself Limited data model, checkout and workflow control The site is commerce-led, membership-heavy or needs deep integrations
Shopify Reliable hosted commerce with a strong admin Polish often relies on paid apps and Shopify's conventions Selling is only a minor feature
Cloudflare Pages Very fast delivery with flexible infrastructure You are running a technical platform, not a self-contained CMS You need low-skill, day-to-day ownership
Netlify Familiar deploys, private previews and built-in forms Usage-based billing and platform-specific features You expect no technical stewardship at all
Vercel Polished, interactive sites that can grow app-like Costs more to run than the other two hosts, in return for top-tier performance and room to grow You only need a simple site, where Cloudflare or Netlify will serve you well
