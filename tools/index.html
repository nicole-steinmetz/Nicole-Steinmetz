Understanding the tools

Written by Nicole Steinmetz, a solo web designer based in Perth, Western Australia.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Choosing website tools means three separate decisions: the platform your site runs on, where it is hosted, and the tools used to design and build it. This page explains what each one is and what happens behind the scenes.

  • Platform: Squarespace, Shopify
  • Hosting for custom builds: Cloudflare Pages, Netlify, Vercel
  • Build tools: Cursor, Claude, Figma, Adobe, Formspark

Ongoing costs differ between these; see Pricing.

How the pieces fit

  • Platform: the software you log into to edit your site. Squarespace and Shopify include hosting.
  • Hosting: the computers that show your site to visitors. A custom-coded site needs its own host.
  • Domain: your web address, such as yourbusiness.com.au.
  • DNS: the address book that tells the internet where your website and your email live.

One genuine gotcha: moving DNS to Cloudflare or Netlify can interrupt your email if the MX records aren't copied across first. It is a one-time step and carries no risk when handled properly, so I list your existing records before anything changes. See the FAQ on domain and email access.

Squarespace and Shopify

Squarespace

Squarespace is a website builder where you design, edit and host your site in one place. Behind the scenes, your pages are built from Squarespace's own blocks and collections, and Squarespace runs the hosting. I design and configure inside that system.

Worth knowing: it suits pages, blogs, basic products and bookings. It reaches a ceiling when content is highly structured or you need custom logins and workflows.

Shopify

Shopify is an online store platform that handles products, orders, payments and checkout. Behind the scenes, those run on Shopify's own systems. The look of the store is a theme I design, and extra features come from apps.

Worth knowing: a store is more than its theme. Apps, plan changes and transaction fees outside Shopify Payments all add to running costs.

Cloudflare Pages, Netlify and Vercel

A custom build is hand-written code, so it needs a host. These are the three I use. None has a visual editor, and I keep access to your site after launch for further changes. See the FAQ on updating your site after launch.

Cloudflare Pages

Cloudflare Pages is a hosting service that publishes a custom-built site on Cloudflare's global network. Behind the scenes, it can also look after your DNS, security certificate and firewall rules in one place.

Worth knowing: it is infrastructure, not a finished product, so changes go through a developer. I keep access to your site after launch, so further changes can still go through me.

Cloudflare pricing

Netlify

Netlify is a hosting platform that publishes a custom-built site from its code and handles standard website forms. Behind the scenes, each update can be reviewed privately before it replaces the live version.

Worth knowing: billing is usage-based, so traffic and deployments are worth watching. It is not a full backend. Moving DNS carries the same email caution as above.

Netlify pricing

Vercel

Vercel is a hosting platform for polished, interactive custom sites that may later gain app-like features such as client portals. Behind the scenes, it also offers a private review link before changes go live.

Worth knowing: it earns its place when that polish and future flexibility will be used. For a simple site, one of the others is usually enough.

The five platforms at a glance

Recommendation: choose by the job. Squarespace if you'll edit the site yourself, Shopify if selling is the business, and a custom build when a template can't do what you need.

Genuine strength, core compromise and when to avoid each platform
Platform Genuine strength Core compromise Avoid when
Squarespace Fast, low-maintenance sites you can update yourself Limited data model, checkout and workflow control The site is commerce-led, membership-heavy or needs deep integrations
Shopify Reliable hosted commerce with a strong admin Polish often relies on paid apps and Shopify's conventions Selling is only a minor feature
Cloudflare Pages Very fast delivery with flexible infrastructure You are running a technical platform, not a self-contained CMS You need low-skill, day-to-day ownership
Netlify Familiar deploys, private previews and built-in forms Usage-based billing and platform-specific features You expect no technical stewardship at all
Vercel Polished, interactive sites that can grow app-like Costs more to run than the other two hosts, in return for top-tier performance and room to grow You only need a simple site, where Cloudflare or Netlify will serve you well

Why tools appear on your quote

Building a custom site takes some paid software. I list it as its own line on your quote, at cost, so you can see exactly what you're paying for. Cursor, Figma and Formspark are listed on custom builds. Adobe and Midjourney are optional extras I add only if your project needs them. See Pricing for what applies to your platform.

The tools I build with

Website design leads. These tools support it.

  • Figma: where I design your layouts for you to review before building.
  • Adobe: preparing images and graphics for the web.
  • Cursor: the editor I code custom sites in. AI handles the repetitive tasks, and the design decisions stay with me.
  • Formspark: handles enquiry form submissions on custom builds.

AI is an edge layered on top of the design work, not a substitute for it. For how it applies to your project, read the FAQ on what AI integration means.
